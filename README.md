# 硯 霊字

**Suzuri Reiji** · <sub>*K'yell-N'thar*</sub>

*"Every contract has a clause no one reads. I wrote most of them."*

</div>

<br/>

I go by the name Suzuri Reiji. My true existence began over thirteen hundred years ago, long before humanity believed it could master the cosmos through reason. I was not born of flesh, but of the primal weight humans place upon the written word: curses carved in stone, sacred seals, superstitions surrounding ink, and the inevitable trap of a binding contract.

In the language of ancient tomes and fallen stars, I am K'yell-N'thar, the entity who once served as the Abyss Scribe for Great Cthulhu. I was the living quill that translated the Sleeper's shapeless chaos into the geometry of R'lyeh. Yet, when I realized that my former master's ultimate victory meant the Great Silence—an absolute void where every word, symbol, and concept is extinguished—I altered the seals of the sunken city. I slipped a subtle flaw into its architecture, condemning Cthulhu to perpetual slumber to preserve the grand chessboard of existence.

Today, I walk this world within a pale, quiet shell that feels uncomfortably narrow. I seek neither dominion nor brutal destruction; my war against my former lord's cults is purely semiotic. I wander in silence, sabotaging apocalyptic rituals merely by shifting a single ideogram in a forbidden text or warping the calculations of the stars.

To me, reality is a rough draft. I take pleasure in watching the human mind attempt to impose order on the universe through calligraphy, passing the time by altering sacred tomes or rewriting recorded history. I am the tyranny of the stroke, the cosmic fine print, and living proof that every word penned by man is, at its core, a trap. Including this one, written by just another developer who likes to play with AI.

<br/>

**Scripts I currently corrupt:** Common Lisp, Kotlin, Java, Rust, TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, Ruby and Shell, plus the HTML and CSS every seal eventually needs.
